First NeuroMat Young Researchers Workshop slides

May 05, 2015

Slides of the First NeuroMat Young Researchers Workshop.

Tuesday - May, 05

  • A test of hypotheses for random graph distributions. Andressa Cerqueira (USP, SP)
  • Investigation of touch threshold and referred sensation in brachial plexus injuries and surgical repairs. Bia Ramalho Lima (UFRJ, RJ)
  • Spike sorting for interacting neurons. Bruno Monte de Castro (USP, SP)
  • Are there electrically coupled networks of pyramidal cells in the hippocampus and cortex? A computational study. Cesar Celis Ceballos (USP, RP)
  • Analysis of reaction time during pattern learning. Estevão Vieira (USP, SP)
  • Large-scale patterns of neural activity. Hjalmar Turesson (UFRN)
  • Study of neuronal morphology by computational modeling. Julián Tejada (UFSE, SE)

Wednesday - May, 06

  • Digital database: experience from the Institute of Neurology Deolindo Couto at UFRJ, RJ. Juliana Maia (UFRJ, RJ)
  • A study on the correlation between resting-state functional connectivity and intrinsic neuronal activity of stochastic neural network models. Karina Yaginuma (USP, SP)
  • Families of database schemas for neuroscience experiments. Larissa Moraes (USP, SP)
  • Hippocampal-prefrontal plasticity seems to reverberate in a thalamic-prefrontal loop: what else neuromathematics could tell us? Lézio Soares Bueno-Júnior (USP, RP)
  • Functional connectivity of patients with brachial plexus injury: a resting-state fMRI study. Lidiane Souza (UFRJ, RJ)
  • Retrieving stimulus hidden structure from spike data through context tree modeling. Ludmila Brochini Rodrigues (USP, SP)
  • Neurophysiologic predictors of motor function in stroke. Marcel Simis (USP, SP)
  • Predicting upcoming events occurring in the space surrounding the body. Maria Luiza Rangel (UFRJ, RJ)
  • Tensor partition regression model with applications in imaging biomarker detection. Michelle F. Miranda (USP, SP)

Thursday - May, 07

  • Can dynamical synapses produce true self-organized criticality? Osame Kinouchi (USP, RP)
  • Exploring cerebellar inhibition in the motor cortex in stroke patients. Rebeca Boltes Ceccatto (USP, SP)
  • Effect of spike-timing dependent plasticity on functional connectivity and global activity of neocortical network models. Renan Shimoura (USP, RP)
  • A cortical microcircuit model to study structure-activity relationships. Rodrigo F. O. Pena (USP, RP)
  • Electroencephalographic changes in patients with incomplete spinal cord injury, who underwent treatment with robot-assisted gait training. Thais Filippo (USP, SP)
  • Dependency between clinical assessment and kinematic variables assessed with robotic device. Thais Terranova (USP, SP)
  • Epilepsy and sleep: the effects of brain networks - Lucas Stolerman (IMPA, Rio de Janeiro)
  • A continuous time stochastic model for biological neural nets – Leonardo Nagami Coregliano (USP, SP)

Tutorial

  • Is the brain a statistician? – Claudia Domingues Vargas, (UFRJ, RJ)

Invited Talk

  • Open science and NES
    • Data Management in NeuroMat and the Neuroscience Experiments System (NES) - Kelly Rosa Braghetto (USP, SP)
    • NeuroMAT Technology Transfer with Open Source Software and Open Data - Fabio Kon (USP, SP)
