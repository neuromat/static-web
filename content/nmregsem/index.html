Neuromat Online Seminars

Apr 20, 2020

  1. About
  2. Poster
  3. Abstracts
  4. Presentation slides

The Research, Innovation and Dissemination Center for Neuromathematics (RIDC NeuroMat) will hold regular online team-wide seminars to present and discuss research progress. Guests and team members will be included in the program.

The series is organized by the RIDC NeuroMat and the University of São Paulo. It is supported by the São Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP). A poster for online dissemination is available.

The program for the NeuroMat Online Seminars is:

  • April 23, 10AM (São Paulo local time): L. Planche (USP) and M. André (USP)
    • title: "What's Metastability (in neural networks)?"
    • room address: meet.google.com/wss-doux-bza
  • May 7, 10AM (São Paulo local time): Rafael B. Stern (UFSCar)
    • title: "Retrieving information from complex data: classifying Parkinson-related variables using the Goal Keeper Game"
    • room address: meet.google.com/gje-vrnn-xub
  • May 21, 10AM (São Paulo local time): C. D. Vargas, O. Baffa and R. Ilmoniemi (Aalto)
    • title: "Structural learning and the brain: new conceptual and methodological venues"
    • room address: meet.google.com/nvo-xizp-cib
  • June 4, 10AM (São Paulo local time): G. Ost (UFRJ)
    • title: "Sparse space-time models"
    • room address: meet.google.com/dad-hmex-qzo

You should check the event page on the NeuroMat portal to make sure the room address has not changed. We will also post eventual changes on our Facebook page.

The NeuroMat Online seminars are meant for a wide audience. If you are not part of any of the research teams involved in organizing the event and have inquiries about the event, please send an email to neuromat@numec.prp.usp.br . We will reply to you as soon as possible.

Please, when you join the room, make sure you have turned off your microphone. It should only be turned on when you want to direct a comment or remark to the event attendees, and then again immediately turned off. This remote-room etiquette is necessary in order to guarantee an efficient communication process.
