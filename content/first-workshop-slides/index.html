First NeuroMat Workshop - slides

Jan 21, 2014

Slides of the First Workshop of FAPESP's Center for Neuromathematics

Monday 20 January

  • Statistical mechanics – Roberto Fernández, Utrecht
  • Neurons and single-unit recording – Sidarta Ribeiro, UFRN
  • Chains with memory of variable lengths – Karina Yuriko Yaginuma, USP
  • Random graphs – Yoshiharu Kohayakawa, USP
  • The scientific project of NeuroMat – Antonio Galves, USP
  • A stochastic model for biological neuronal nets – Eva Löcherbach, Cergy-Pontoise
  • A system of interacting neurons stops spiking without external stimuli – Aline Duarte de Oliveira, USP

Tuesday 21 January

  • NeuroMat technology transfer, open source software, and open data – Fabio Kon, USP
  • Fast generation of context tree models – Arnaldo Mandel, USP
  • Rich-club organization of the human connectome – Martijn van den Heuvel, Utrecht
  • EEG / ECoG – Guillermo Cecchi, IBM
  • DTI, fMRI – Fátima Erthal, UFRJ
  • Brain plasticity – Felipe Fregni, Harvard
  • Development of NeuroMat open databases – Kelly Rosa Braghetto, USP
  • General issues and the experience of constructing a clinical database in a university hospital – Cláudia Domingues Vargas, UFRJ

Wednesday 22 January

  • Sandpiles on Epileptic Brain – Abner Rodrigues, USP
  • Predictors of Motor Recovery – Marcel Simis, IRLM
  • Stochastic modeling of behavioral data – André Frazão Helene, USP
  • Stochastic modeling of neurophysiological and behavioral data: attention and decision making in rats – Gilberto Xavier, USP

Thursday 23 January

  • Visually Driven Functional Networks in the Brain – Cláudia Domingues Vargas, UFRJ
  • Statistical analysis of random graphs applied to neuroscience – Florencia Leonardi, USP
  • Processing Multielectrode EEG Signals – Jorge Stolfi, UNICAMP
  • Pydio: A powerful file-sharing platform – Carlos Eduardo Ribas, USP

Friday 24 January

  • Dynamical Criticality: Theory and Application to ECoG – Guillermo Cecchi, IBM
  • How do epileptic seizures begin? The contribution of brain-state transitions – Claudio Queiroz, UFRN
  • Statistical inference for time varying networks – Roberto Imbuzeiro Oliveira, IMPA
  • Classifying EEG data driven by rhythmic stimuli – Guilherme Ost, USP
  • From the intrinsic properties to the functional role of a neuron phenotype – Angel Caputi, IIBCE
